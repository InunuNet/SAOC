Ticket transfers
If you are unable to attend, you may transfer your ticket to another person. Please provide the new attendee with a printed copy of the original ticket, including its QR code. The printed ticket must be presented at registration for scanning.
Each QR code is valid for one ticket entitlement only and may not be used by more than one person.
Cancellation and refund policy
Cancellation requests must be submitted in writing to the organisers, quoting your booking reference.
Refunds are calculated on the original ticket price, less the applicable cancellation percentage and a 5% administration fee:
- More than 60 days before the events: 90% refund, less the 5% administration fee (85% of the original ticket price).
- 30–60 days before the events: 75% refund, less the 5% administration fee (70% of the original ticket price).
- 14–29 days before the events: 50% refund, less the 5% administration fee (45% of the original ticket price).
- Less than 14 days before the events: Requests will be considered individually. Any approved refund will be subject to the 5% administration fee.
The administration fee covers transaction and processing costs incurred by the organisers, including payment gateway and bank charges.
If the organisers cancel either event, the organisers will communicate the arrangements applicable to the affected ticket entitlement. If both events are cancelled, affected ticket holders will receive a full refund without an administration fee.
Approved refunds will be processed within 14 business days.
