- Ticket holder: {visitor_name}

- Payment status: {payment_status}
Your ticket and entry
Your ticket is valid for one day only, on the date selected when making your booking.
Please bring your ticket, either printed or displayed on your mobile device, so that the QR code can be scanned at the entrance.
Exhibition opening hours
- Friday, 24 September 2027: 09:00–17:00
- Saturday, 25 September 2027: 09:00–17:00
- Sunday, 26 September 2027: 09:00–16:00
Venue and parking
Hangar 25, Stellenbosch Airfield, Stellenbosch, Western Cape (R44).
Parking will be available at the venue. Further parking and access instructions will be provided closer to the event.
Ticket transfers
If you are unable to attend, you may transfer your ticket to another person. Please give the new ticket holder a printed copy of the original ticket, including its QR code. The printed ticket must be presented at the entrance for scanning.
Each QR code is valid for one ticket entitlement only and may not be used by more than one person.
Cancellation and refund policy
Cancellation requests must be submitted in writing to the organisers, quoting your booking reference.
Refunds are calculated on the original ticket price, less the applicable cancellation percentage and a 5% administration fee:
- More than 60 days before the event: 90% refund, less the 5% administration fee (85% of the original ticket price).
- 30–60 days before the event: 75% refund, less the 5% administration fee (70% of the original ticket price).
- 14–29 days before the event: 50% refund, less the 5% administration fee (45% of the original ticket price).
- Less than 14 days before the event: Requests will be considered individually. Any approved refund will be subject to the 5% administration fee.
The administration fee covers transaction and processing costs incurred by the organisers, including payment gateway and bank charges.
If the organisers cancel the event, affected ticket holders will receive a full refund without an administration fee.
Approved refunds will be processed within 14 business days.
We look forward to seeing you!
For further information, please visit nos.saoc.co.za or contact us at info@saoc.co.za.
Kind regards,
The National Orchid Show – Western Cape 2027 Organising Committee

A few backend points to note
- The SAOC/WOSA Joint Ticket should generate one booking confirmation but provide access to both Friday and Saturday events.
- The QR code should validate separately for each event, rather than being marked as fully used after Friday.
- If a booking includes more than one attendee, the system should ideally generate an individual QR code for each attendee.
- The joint ticket cancellation wording needs to distinguish between cancellation of one event and cancellation of both, as reflected above.
I have used the Friday and Saturday programme dates from the earlier event planning. The specific venues and registration times have deliberately been left out until those details are confirmed.
