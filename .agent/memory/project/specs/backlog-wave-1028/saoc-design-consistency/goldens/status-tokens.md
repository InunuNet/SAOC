SocietiesClient's wrapper is allowlisted as 'wrapper carries the ring via focus-within' only if the dev confirms it does; otherwise it converts. Supplementary cues that are not outline or ring (group-focus underline and decoration on EventCard and PartnersSection, focus-visible:bg-*, focus:border-*, the AdminShell skip link's focus:bg-*) are ignored by the check, not listed. NOS-only files (components/tickets/TicketTypeCard.tsx, components/show/*, components/nos/*, components/nos-chrome/*, app/(nos)/*) are excluded. Gate: A4 (runtime probes plus check-focus-classes-static.mjs: it enumerates every .ts/.tsx className and fails on an unlisted hit or a stale entry).
