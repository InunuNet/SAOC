A Conference held by Wild Orchids Southern Africa (WOSA) will highlight the ecology and conservation of indigenous orchids, illustrating how research undertaken in natural habitats continues to influence cultivation practices, breeding programmes and the long-term future of orchids.

An important feature of the 2027 programme will be the participation of Wild Orchids of Southern Africa (WOSA). They will hold their annual conference as part of the National Orchid Show. Their involvement reflects the National Orchid Show's commitment to connecting conservation science with horticultural excellence, demonstrating how knowledge gained from orchids in the wild continues to shape responsible cultivation and the future sustainability of the orchid community.

Frequently asked questions

What is WOSA?

WOSA stands for Wild Orchids Southern Africa, an organisation focused on South Africa's wild orchids and their conservation.

Do I have to be a WOSA member to attend?

No, anyone can attend.

What is included in the WOSA Conference ticket?

The conference and refrenshments.

Will the conference include speakers?

Yes, a speaker programme is planned. The final speakers, presentation topics and timetable will be announced once confirmed.

Will refreshments and lunch be provided?

Refreshments will be provided for all attendees. However not all food allergies and preferences can be accommodated.

Is the WOSA Conference accessible to wheelchair users?

Yes, the venue is accessible.

How do I register for the WOSA Conference?

Via the National Show website and ticket sales table.
