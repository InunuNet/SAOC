About the show

Three years in the making,
four days on the bench

The SAOC National Orchid Show is the country's premier competitive orchid event. Held every three years, it rotates across South Africa's nine provinces, bringing together growers, hybridisers, judges and collectors for four days of competition, education and community.

Every plant is assessed by accredited SAOC judges against the 100-point scale across ten botanical classes. The Grand Champion is the highest-scoring exhibit across all classes.

Competition structure

Ten judging groups

Every exhibit is entered in one of ten botanical classes. Judges score on a 100-point scale covering cultural quality, presentation and species accuracy. Four columns, not five — a card carrying four text elements needs about 280px, and five across this container leaves 230.

C
Group 1

Cattleya Alliance

Cattleya, Laelia, Brassavola, Rhyncholaelia and intergeneric hybrids.

Cy
Group 2

Cymbidium

Standard, miniature, pendulous and species Cymbidium.

D
Group 3

Dendrobium

Den-Phal, hard-cane, soft-cane, nobile type and species.

m
Group 4

Miniatures

Plants with flowers under 4cm at widest point.

N
Group 5

Novice

Growers who have not won a first prize at SAOC level.

P
Group 6

Phalaenopsis

Standard, novelty and species Phalaenopsis.

Pa
Group 7

Paphiopedilum

Slipper orchids, complex hybrids and species.

V
Group 8

Vanda Alliance

Vanda, Ascocentrum, Renanthera and intergenerics.

S
Group 9

Species

True species of any genus, shown as collected or cultivated.

Di
Group 10

Disa & SA Terrestrials

Cape terrestrials including Disa and allied genera.

Entering the show

Exhibitor information

Stage 01

Register your interest

Contact your provincial society to express exhibitor interest. Early registration secures bench space and priority catalogue listing.

Stage 02

Confirm entry and classes

Submit your entry form with plant list and class selections. Entries reviewed against current SAOC judging standards.

Stage 03

Staging and preparation

Arrive at the venue for bench set-up from early morning. All plants must be in place and labelled before the close of staging.

Stage 04

Judging and awards

SAOC accredited judges assess each class over the opening days, followed by the awards ceremony.
