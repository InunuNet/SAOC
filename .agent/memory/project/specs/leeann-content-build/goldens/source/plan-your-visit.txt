**PLAN YOUR VISIT**

**Discover Stellenbosch, Cape Town, the Cape Winelands and the greater Western Cape**

**Join us for the National Orchid Show – Western Cape 2027!**

Welcome to the Western Cape, South Africa — a region renowned for its spectacular mountain scenery, historic towns, beautiful gardens, diverse plant life and world-class hospitality.

The National Orchid Show will take place in Stellenbosch, in the heart of the Cape Winelands. Whether you are travelling from elsewhere in South Africa or joining us from overseas, we invite you to make the most of your visit by exploring the region before or after the show.

From the historic streets and vineyards of Stellenbosch to the iconic landscapes and attractions of Cape Town, there is plenty to discover. Use our travel guide below to plan your journey, find accommodation and explore the many experiences the Western Cape has to offer.

**1. Getting Here**

**By air**

The main international gateway to the Western Cape is **Cape Town International Airport (CPT)**, which serves domestic, regional and international travellers.

Stellenbosch is approximately 30 km from the airport, making it convenient to combine the National Orchid Show with a holiday in Cape Town and the surrounding Winelands. Travel times will depend on traffic and your chosen transport.

We recommend arranging your onward transport before travelling, particularly if you are arriving during a busy event period.

**Useful links**

* [Cape Town International Airport — official airport information](https://www.airports.co.za/airports/cape-town-international-airport)
* [Flight information and airport services](https://www.airports.co.za/)
* [Google Flights — compare flight options](https://www.google.com/travel/flights)
* [Skyscanner — compare flights](https://www.skyscanner.net/)

**Getting from the airport to Stellenbosch**

Visitors can reach Stellenbosch by rental car, pre-arranged private transfer, shuttle or taxi/ride-ehailing service. If you plan to explore Cape Town and the Winelands independently, hiring a car may offer greater flexibility.

There is also a MyCiTi airport bus service to central Cape Town. If you are travelling directly to Stellenbosch, check your onward connections carefully, as you may need an additional transfer.

* [Cape Town International Airport — transport and travel information](https://www.airports.co.za/airports/cape-town-international-airport)

*Please check current routes, fares, operating hours and transfer arrangements closer to your travel date.*

**2. Where to Stay**

September is a wonderful time to explore the Cape, and the National Orchid Show is an opportunity to enjoy both Stellenbosch and Cape Town. We encourage visitors to book accommodation early, especially if they intend to stay over the show weekend.

**Stay in Stellenbosch**

Stellenbosch offers a wide choice of accommodation, from hotels and guesthouses to self-catering apartments, country lodges and vineyard retreats.

Staying in or near the historic town centre allows you to explore its oak-lined streets, cafés, restaurants, galleries and heritage buildings. Accommodation in the surrounding Winelands offers a more rural setting, with vineyards and mountain scenery.

Explore available accommodation through the following websites:

* [Visit Stellenbosch — official destination and accommodation guide](https://www.visitstellenbosch.org/stay/)
* [Booking.com — Stellenbosch accommodation](https://www.booking.com/searchresults.html?ss=Stellenbosch%2C%20South%20Africa)

* [LekkeSlaap — South African accommodation](https://www.lekkeslaap.co.za/)
* [SA-Venues — South African accommodation](https://www.sa-venues.com/)

**Stay in Cape Town**

Cape Town is an excellent base for visitors who would like to combine the orchid show with sightseeing, beaches, museums, shopping and some of South Africa's best-known attractions.

The city offers accommodation to suit a wide range of budgets, from hotels and guesthouses to apartments and holiday homes. Popular areas include the City Bowl, the V&A Waterfront, the Atlantic Seaboard and the Southern Suburbs.

Explore your options:

**Which is the best place to stay?**

If the orchid show is your main reason for visiting, we recommend considering accommodation in Stellenbosch or its surrounding areas. If you would like to spend several days sightseeing in Cape Town as well, you could divide your stay between the two destinations.

Please note that accommodation providers operate independently of the National Orchid Show. Visitors should confirm prices, availability, cancellation conditions, transport arrangements and the distance from the show venue before booking.

**3. Discover Stellenbosch**

Welcome to the heart of the Cape Winelands!

Founded in 1679, Stellenbosch is one of South Africa's oldest towns and is celebrated for its historic architecture, university atmosphere, art galleries, restaurants, wine estates and beautiful natural surroundings.

The town is an attraction in its own right. Spend time wandering through its historic centre, exploring local shops and galleries, visiting museums and gardens, or enjoying the scenery of the surrounding vineyards and mountains.

**Things to see and do**

**History and heritage**

Explore the town's historic streets and Cape Dutch architecture, and discover the stories that have shaped Stellenbosch over the centuries.

**Gardens and botanical interest**

Plant lovers may enjoy visiting the Stellenbosch University Botanical Garden, a botanical collection in the centre of town. It offers an opportunity to explore plant diversity in a peaceful garden setting.

**Art, culture and local shopping**

Browse art galleries, craft shops, boutiques and local markets, and enjoy the town's cafés and restaurants.

**The Cape Winelands**

The countryside around Stellenbosch is known for its vineyards, mountain scenery, wine estates and food experiences. Visitors can arrange guided excursions, cellar tours, tastings and scenic drives.

**Outdoor activities**

Walking, cycling and nature experiences provide opportunities to enjoy the landscapes surrounding Stellenbosch.

**Plan your Stellenbosch visit**

* [Visit Stellenbosch — official tourism website](https://www.visitstellenbosch.org/)
* [Plan your visit to Stellenbosch](https://www.visitstellenbosch.org/plan-your-visit/)
* [Stellenbosch accommodation](https://www.visitstellenbosch.org/stay/)
* [Cape Town Tourism's guide to Stellenbosch](https://www.capetown.travel/neighbourhood/stellenbosch/)
* [Stellenbosch University Botanical Garden](https://www.sun.ac.za/english/faculty/science/conservation-ecology/botanical-garden)

We recommend allowing some extra time to explore Stellenbosch rather than treating it simply as the location of the show.

**4. Discover Cape Town**

No visit to the Western Cape would be complete without experiencing Cape Town, one of South Africa's most iconic destinations.

Set between Table Mountain and the Atlantic Ocean, Cape Town offers a remarkable combination of natural beauty, history, culture, beaches, gardens, restaurants and attractions.

Whether you have one day or a few extra days to explore, there is something for everyone.

**Highlights of Cape Town**

**Table Mountain**

Enjoy one of the country's most recognisable landmarks. Depending on weather conditions and operating schedules, visitors can explore hiking routes or take the cableway.

**Kirstenbosch National Botanical Garden**

Situated on the eastern slopes of Table Mountain, Kirstenbosch is a highlight for garden lovers and anyone interested in South Africa's indigenous flora.

**V&A Waterfront**

Explore the waterfront's shops, restaurants, harbour views and attractions. It is also a departure point for some boat trips and excursions.

**Robben Island**

Discover an important part of South Africa's history through a guided visit to the former prison where Nelson Mandela was incarcerated. Advance reservations are advisable.

**Cape Point and the Cape Peninsula**

Explore dramatic coastal scenery, nature reserves, historic sites and spectacular viewpoints on a day trip around the Cape Peninsula.

**Beaches and coastal villages**

From Camps Bay and Clifton to the charming coastal communities of the peninsula, visitors can enjoy beautiful ocean views and a variety of seaside experiences.

**Plan your Cape Town visit**

* [Cape Town Tourism — official visitor guide](https://www.capetown.travel/)
* [Cape Town maps and visitor guides](https://www.capetown.travel/plan-your-trip/cape-town-maps-and-guides/)
* [Kirstenbosch National Botanical Garden](https://www.sanbi.org/gardens/kirstenbosch/)
* [Table Mountain Aerial Cableway](https://www.tablemountain.net/)
* [Robben Island Museum](https://www.robben-island.org.za/)
* [SANParks — Table Mountain National Park](https://www.sanparks.org/parks/table-mountain)

Cape Town is approximately 50 km from Stellenbosch, so it is possible to combine the two destinations during your stay. Allow sufficient time for travel, particularly during peak traffic periods.

**5. Explore Beyond Stellenbosch and Cape Town**

If your schedule allows, consider extending your visit to discover more of the Western Cape.

The surrounding region offers historic towns, mountain passes, nature reserves, coastal scenery, botanical gardens and the landscapes of the Cape Winelands.

You might consider visiting:

* **Franschhoek** — known for its heritage, food, art and surrounding vineyards.
* **Paarl** — with its historic sites, mountain scenery and wine estates.
* **Hermanus** — a coastal destination known for its marine scenery and seasonal whale-watching opportunities.
* **The Cape Peninsula** — for coastal drives, nature reserves and dramatic ocean views.

For ideas and suggested itineraries, visit [Cape Town Tourism](https://www.capetown.travel/) and [Visit Stellenbosch](https://www.visitstellenbosch.org/).

**6. Practical Information for International Visitors**

**Travel documents and visas**

International visitors should check passport validity, visa requirements and any applicable entry conditions well in advance of travel. Requirements depend on nationality and can change.

Please consult the official South African government website for the latest information:

[South African Government — visa applications and requirements](https://www.gov.za/services/temporary-residence/visa)

**Weather in September**

September marks the beginning of spring in the Western Cape. Conditions can vary, with pleasant sunny days as well as cooler, windy or rainy weather.

We recommend bringing layers of clothing and a light waterproof jacket, particularly if you intend to explore gardens, vineyards or mountain areas.

Check the forecast closer to your arrival:

[South African Weather Service](https://www.weathersa.co.za/)

**Currency and payments**

The currency used in South Africa is the South African rand (ZAR). Bank cards are widely accepted, although it is useful to have an alternative payment method available for smaller purchases.

**Getting around**

If you intend to visit multiple destinations, plan your transport in advance. Distances, traffic, parking and the availability of public transport can affect how much you can fit into a day.

If you plan to enjoy wine tastings, consider booking a guided tour or arranging a designated driver.

**Visitor information**

For local advice, activities, maps and help planning excursions, consult the official destination organisations:

* [Visit Stellenbosch](https://www.visitstellenbosch.org/)
* [Cape Town Tourism](https://www.capetown.travel/)

**7. Make the Most of Your Visit**

The National Orchid Show is an opportunity to celebrate orchids, meet fellow enthusiasts and discover the diversity of South African and international orchid growing.

We encourage you to allow a little extra time to experience the region's gardens, landscapes, history and hospitality. Whether you choose to explore Stellenbosch on foot, enjoy the Winelands, visit Cape Town's iconic attractions or extend your journey along the coast, the Western Cape offers plenty of reasons to stay longer.

We look forward to welcoming you to Stellenbosch for the **National Orchid Show – Western Cape 2027**.
