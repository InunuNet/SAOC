=== ARCHITECT FIX 2026-09-06 | check-refunds-provisional-figures.mjs: spelled-out figures ===

Reported by @dev via team lead: A4's FIGURE_PATTERN required a DIGIT inside every
data-provisional-figure mark, but A12 (legacy check-refunds-no-fabrication.mjs) bans
digit-based day/hour/week figures anywhere on the page (its %-boundary bug only exempts
percentages, not day/hour/week — that exemption was mis-stated as covering all units in
the original golden §8). The two assertions were unsatisfiable together for any
day/hour/week threshold: A4 demanded the digit be present inside the mark, A12 demanded
it be absent from the page, same string, no arrangement satisfies both.

Reproduced the exact failure dev reported:
$ node contracts/checks/policy-pages/check-refunds-provisional-figures.mjs --fixture .tmp/sandbox/refunds-arch/dev-reported-mark.html
  (fixture: <mark data-provisional-figure="true">thirty days<sup>provisional</sup></mark>)
FAIL: provisional-figure region #1 does not carry both a real figure and the word
"provisional" as visible text: "thirty days provisional"
exit: 1 (confirmed the bug before fixing)

Fix: extended FIGURE_PATTERN / FIGURE_PATTERN_G in check-refunds-provisional-figures.mjs
to recognise a spelled-out number word (one..ninety, with an optional hyphenated/spaced
ones-word after a tens-word) adjacent to day/hour/week as a real figure, alongside the
existing digit-based and %-based alternatives. Applied to BOTH the "bare figure outside
a mark" check and the "mark contains a real figure" check, so a bare spelled-out figure
sitting outside a mark is still caught (the fabrication guard is not weakened).
