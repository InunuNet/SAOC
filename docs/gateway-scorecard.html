Three things to know before you read the ranking

Nobody has an account anywhere yet. The website has a test-mode PayFast connection built by the developer, but SAOC holds no merchant account with any of these five. Every provider starts from zero, and every one needs FICA paperwork before it can take money.

The deadline is the end of August 2026 — about two weeks. Whichever provider is chosen has to get through sign-up, entity verification and activation inside that window. This is why "how fast can we actually open an account" is one of the eleven criteria and not a footnote.

One unanswered question could change the winner. PayFast is the only provider with a non-profit product — a "Cause account" with reduced fees. It is advertised for donations, and SAOC sells tickets, not donations. Nobody publishes the Cause rate; the only figure in writing anywhere is a one-day promotion from 2018. If that discount does reach ticket sales, PayFast goes from the most expensive of the five to comfortably the cheapest. That is a question for PayFast, and it is worth asking before the Council votes.

What the numbers are built on

  • Cost is the fee on one R500 card ticket, before VAT, at each provider's published standard rate: Ozow R14.25, Paystack R15.50, Peach R16.25, Yoco R16.75, PayFast R18.00. Across 2,000 tickets that is a spread of roughly R7,500 between cheapest and dearest — real money, but less than a single bad clause could cost.
  • The advertised-versus-signed gap is the Council's own test: does the contract you sign actually commit the provider to the price on the website? PayFast fails this hardest — its general terms contain no fee amounts at all, deferring every charge to an application form you don't see until you apply. Paystack does best: a published rate with a worked example and the VAT basis stated.
  • Lock-in and dormancy matter far more here than for a normal business, because the account sits idle for about 36 months between shows. Yoco's terms say an inactive profile simply stays inactive. PayFast's suspend at six months and auto-terminate at twelve, with a fee.
  • Card machines are a real operational difference over three show days. Yoco is the only one of the five that supplies them, so door and stall sales run on the same account as online tickets.
  • Customer data is where Ozow stands out badly: its terms have SAOC consent, on its ticket buyers' behalf, to Ozow marketing to them directly and to their data being processed outside South Africa. It is written as opt-out. SAOC cannot give that consent for its members, and whether the clause is enforceable is a question for a lawyer, not for us — but no other provider's terms ask for it.
