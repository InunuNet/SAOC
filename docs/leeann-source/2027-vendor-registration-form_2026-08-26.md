2027 SAOC NATIONAL ORCHID SHOW

VENDOR REGISTRATION FORM AND AGREEMENT
We are pleased to welcome you as a pre-approved vendor at the 2027 SAOC National Orchid Show.
This Vendor Registration Form and Agreement is intended to collect the information required by the Show Organising Committee to plan and manage your participation in the show. The information provided will be used for booth allocation, exhibitor and staff access, equipment and utility requirements, deliveries and logistics, security, marketing and programme information, and any permits, certificates or regulatory requirements applicable to your business or activities.
Please complete all applicable sections of this form in full and provide any supporting documentation requested. Accurate and complete information is essential to enable the organisers to plan the vendor area safely and efficiently and to meet the operational, venue and regulatory requirements of the event.
This form also serves as the formal agreement between the vendor and the Show Organising Committee in respect of participation in the 2027 SAOC National Orchid Show. By completing and submitting this form, the vendor confirms that the information provided is accurate and complete, agrees to comply with the requirements applicable to their participation, and accepts the 2027 SAOC National Show Vendor Terms and Conditions contained in this document.
Completion and submission of this form, together with the required payment and supporting documentation, confirms the vendor's participation and acceptance of the terms and conditions governing their involvement in the 2027 SAOC National Orchid Show.
