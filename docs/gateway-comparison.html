Things the scoring cannot capture

Only Peach puts the price in the contract. Its rates sit in Annexure A of the signed agreement, changeable only by mutual agreement. Ozow, Yoco and Paystack all defer to their public pricing pages — visible, but alterable. PayFast defers to a private "Application" you cannot see until you apply, and its clause 3.4 commits you to a Minimum Volume Fee whenever monthly volume is R20,000 or under, with the amount disclosed nowhere. For a show held once every three years, that threshold is unmet in roughly 35 months out of 36.

Yoco's own two contracts disagree with each other. The 2020 Merchant Agreement requires binding arbitration in Cape Town; the 2026 Main T&Cs says no arbitration and any court. The liability cap differs too. Ask Yoco which document governs before signing anything.

Yoco has no 3-D Secure statement for online checkout. All five are confirmed PASA-registered from the regulator's own public register — that part is independently verified, not marketing. But Yoco is the only one with no discoverable 3DS commitment for card-not-present payments; its security material is all about card machines. SAOC's ticket sales are 100% online, and 3DS is what shifts fraud liability away from you. Ask before signing.

It compounds: Mastercard publishes a register of every service provider that has filed a PCI Attestation of Compliance, with the auditor's name and date. Ozow, PayFast, Peach and Paystack are all on it. Yoco is not. That is a checkable absence, not proof of non-compliance — Yoco may rely on its acquiring bank's certification, or simply not submit to Mastercard's list. But it is the one registry that could actually be queried, and Yoco is missing from it while its four competitors are present. Ask Yoco directly for its PCI status and its AOC.

Two caveats on the others. Paystack's attestation is filed under Paystack, Inc., the global entity — not confirmed as covering Paystack South Africa, which is who you would contract with; and its own compliance page cites PCI DSS 3.2, an older standard than Peach's stated 4.0.1. Every provider's level and version remains its own word: Mastercard's list records that an attestation exists, not what it says. Ask each for the document.

Nobody has an account anywhere. The PayFast integration on the website is a developer sandbox, not an SAOC account. All five start from zero, all five need FICA, and the deadline is the end of August — about two weeks. Not one of the five publishes a verification turnaround time, so "can we actually be live" is the one factor no amount of further research will settle. It needs an email to each.

One unanswered question could move PayFast a long way. It is the only provider with a non-profit product — a Cause account with "reduced fees" — but the rate is published nowhere, and the marketing is written around donations while SAOC sells tickets. If that discount reaches ticket sales, PayFast goes from dearest to cheapest. Worth an email before you decide.

What to ask each provider, in writing

  • All five: how long does verification take for a registered NPO, from submission to live? None publish it.
  • PayFast: what is the Minimum Volume Fee amount, does the Cause rate apply to ticket sales rather than donations, and what is that rate? Also: clause 21.2(iii) refers to a Termination Fee in clause 21.3(ii), which does not exist in the document — what is the fee?
  • Yoco: which contract governs, the 2020 Merchant Agreement or the 2026 Main T&Cs? They conflict on arbitration and on the liability cap.
  • Ozow: clause 20.8.1 has SAOC consent, on its ticket buyers' behalf, to Ozow marketing to them and to their data being processed across borders, on an opt-out basis. Can it be waived? SAOC cannot give that consent for its members.
  • Peach: what is the KYC turnaround, is there a bespoke NPO rate, and is there a cap on how long funds can be held during a dispute? The contract sets none.
  • Paystack: the contract allows termination on 24 hours' notice and disclaims any uptime guarantee. What happens if that falls during the show weekend?
